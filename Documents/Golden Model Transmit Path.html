2  Call sequence, one frame

Why the chain ends in two files, not one. writeVectors emits tx_stim.txt — what the testbench feeds the design — and tx_expected.hex, the cycles the design must produce. Both come from the same model run, so the stimulus and the expectation cannot disagree about what frame was sent. The testbench drives one, captures the pins, and diffs against the other.

3  Where each block can be wrong

Every one of these produces a model that is internally consistent and still wrong — which is why the validation for the CRC comes from outside this codebase entirely.

BlockThe failure that does not announce itselfCaught by
params A constant parsed wrong, or a stale cache serving values from before the header was edited. The model then disagrees with the RTL about something like the minimum frame length, and the mismatch looks exactly like a design bug. tParams × 7
crc32 Reflection applied at the wrong stage. Produces a self-consistent CRC that round-trips perfectly against itself and fails every real receiver. check value 0xCBF43926
zlib × 2000
fcsBytes Octet order reversed. The frame looks correct in every log you own and is rejected by every NIC on the wire. tFrame/fcsIsLeast­SignificantOctetFirst
buildFrame FCS coverage off by eight — including preamble and SFD, which are part of the packet but not the frame. Also EtherType, the one field that is big-endian at octet granularity while every octet still ships LSB-bit-first. tFrame × 18
abortedFrame Padding an aborted frame, or emitting an FCS that merely differs rather than one guaranteed to fail. Either makes the RTL chase behaviour the standard never asked for. tAbort × 10
buildStream Emitting only the nominal 12-octet gap. A receiver must survive 8 (Table 4-2 Note 3), so a regression that never shrinks the gap passes even when RX recovery silently needs 15 cycles — and that bug debuts on hardware. rx_min_gap scenario
rgmiiEncode The falling-edge control XOR backwards. Yields a MAC that carries clean traffic perfectly and never reports an error. tRgmii/control­EncodingTable
The pattern. Every failure above is silent by construction — it produces output that is plausible, self-consistent, and wrong. That is why crc32 is validated against a published constant and a different language's implementation rather than against itself, and why the generator reads its own wire back with the golden receive path before it will write a vector file.
